PLAYING AS A GUEST

You can play without an account. Your scores, stats and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

IF YOU CREATE AN ACCOUNT

We store your email address, the player name you choose, your best scores and game statistics. They are kept with Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) so you can sign in, sync progress and appear on the leaderboard.

  • Your player name and best scores are visible to other players on the leaderboard.
  • If you add a profile picture while signed in, a small thumbnail of it is shown next to your score on the leaderboard. You can remove it at any time from your profile.
  • Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking.

DELETING YOUR DATA

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your saved progress and your leaderboard entries. You can also email us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it for you.

WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers. Your profile picture stays on your device unless you are signed in, in which case only a small thumbnail is stored for the leaderboard.
